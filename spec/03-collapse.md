## Scope

How a learned document is generalized: the path analyzer and its thresholds, per-binary collapse configuration, exec-argument generalization (grouping by binary, argument tries, argv0 shape collapse, the `[argv0, ⋯⋯]` fallback), open and endpoint collapse, proc-identifier folding, CIDR aggregation, idempotence of a second collapse, and the rule that user-managed documents are stored as applied and never re-collapsed.
